The Codex of Honor

A Knight's Words for a World in Need

My Personal Code

Justice Before Judgment: I will seek truth before issuing blame. If punishment must fall, it will fall with purpose.

Compassion Is Not Weakness: I will not mistake cruelty for discipline. The weak deserve protection.

The Spirit, Not Just the Words: I will live by the Measure's heart—even when its wording falls short of wisdom.

Mercy Is a Blade of Its Own: I will spare those who yield. Every foe was once a child.

I Serve the People: My loyalty is to those who suffer, not those who rule.

Stand Alone When Necessary: Honor means choosing the hard path when it is the right one.

The Knightly Vow

I vow to stand between the innocent and the shadow.
I will act with courage, tempered by compassion.
I will hold truth above comfort, and mercy above pride.
I will fight with faith, but I will never let faith blind me to suffering.
Let my sword be swift against evil, and my shield steadfast for the helpless.
This I swear, in honor of my mentor and in service to all who cannot lift a blade of their own.
